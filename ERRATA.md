# Errata

Confirmed corrections to *Learning with AI*, listed with the version in which the error was identified. Four corrections were identified in v4.49.0 to v4.49.1 by reading cited sources at source; all are already applied in v4.49.2 and are listed here because readers of earlier drafts saw the uncorrected text.

## How to report a correction

Use the [feedback form](FEEDBACK.md) (no account needed) or, on GitHub, open a [correction issue](https://github.com/machyman/hyman2026learning/issues/new/choose). Please include the page number and the version, which appears in the PDF filename (for example, `learning_with_ai_v4_49_2.pdf`). The draft's title page carries a build date rather than a version.

## Reference status notes

The book cites a fast-moving 2025 to 2026 evidence base. This section records the status of time-sensitive references after publication, so that the bibliography's dated snapshot entries age visibly rather than silently.

An entry appears here when a cited work changes status: a preprint is published, withdrawn, or revised; a reported figure is corrected; a platform feature the book describes is discontinued.

| Reference | As cited in the book | Status update | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lin, *Hidden Prompts in Manuscripts Exploit AI-Assisted Peer Review* | arXiv preprint 2507.06185 (2025) | Published in *Communications of the ACM* 69(7), 53-56, 25 June 2026, doi:10.1145/3779116. arXiv now carries a v2 dated 17 August 2026. The claim the book draws from it is unchanged | 2026-08-26 |

If you know of a status change to a work the book cites, please report it (see [FEEDBACK.md](FEEDBACK.md)). This is exactly the kind of correction that keeps a book honest between editions.
