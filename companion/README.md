# Companion prompts and worked artifacts

Reusable prompts and worked examples from *Learning with AI: A Framework for Students, Instructors,
and Universities* by James M. Hyman (SIAM Books).

These are shared across audiences. The student and instructor materials live in
[`students/`](../students/README.md) and [`instructors/`](../instructors/README.md).

| Material | Who it is for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [spp/](spp/README.md) | Students | Study Partner Protocol: a paste-in prompt that turns an assistant into a study partner |
| [dpp/](dpp/README.md) | Students | Define Personal Preferences: a walkthrough for configuring an AI tool's persistent settings |
| [layered-explanation/](layered-explanation/README.md) | Students | A prompt that explains a hard concept at three levels at once: an everyday analogy, your working level, and one step deeper |
| [compact/](compact/README.md) | Students | The five-part Student Compact from Part I, printable and signable |
| [course-case-study/](course-case-study/README.md) | Instructors | A first numerical-analysis course designed end to end with the framework: syllabus AI policy, three assignments, an in-person exam, and the reasoning behind each choice |
| [instructor-capabilities/](instructor-capabilities/README.md) | Instructors | A working guide to AI tools for teaching preparation: what each is good for, how to use it well, what a good result looks like, and how to obtain current ones |
| [spiral-problems/](spiral-problems/README.md) | Instructors | A method for recasting single-answer problems so that working them runs the Learning Spiral, with worked before-and-after pairs from a writing course, a data course, and a first-year lab |
